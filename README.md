## **CO**ntinous **L**earning capabilities for func**T**ional safety **R**un-time thre**A**ts ma**N**ag**E**ment in Automotive RISC**-V** based ECU

**COLTRANE-V** is a PRIN 2022 project (2022HWM3T9, 2023-2025) coordinated by **[Politecnico di Torino](https://www.polito.it)** and funded by the **[Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR)](https://www.mur.gov.it)**.

The project aims to improve the dependability of automotive ECUs through a **continuous learning** approach: fault and attack detection together with real-time countermeasures on a RISC-V architecture with an AI accelerator, in collaboration with the **University of Catania** and the **University of Genoa**.
